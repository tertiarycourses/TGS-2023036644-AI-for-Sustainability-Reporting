# Activity 5 Determine material topics with GRI 3

**Learning outcome:** LO2  
**Duration:** 75 minutes  
**Real use case:** CDL 2025 double materiality process  

## Objective

Identify, assess and prioritise impacts using the GRI 3 significance process.

## Files

- `case-study.pdf` - scenario, evidence and discussion tasks
- `quiz.pdf` - five open-ended knowledge checks
- `worksheet.csv` - working evidence register

## Inputs

CDL topic list, impact register and severity worksheet

## Detailed procedure

1. Review organisational context and business relationships.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
2. Write impact statements using cause-pathway-subject-consequence.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
3. Score scale, scope and irremediable character.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
4. Assess likelihood only for potential impacts.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
5. Apply the threshold and document governance validation.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.

## Expected output

Prioritised material-topic register and decision log

## Verification

High-severity human-rights impacts are not screened out by low likelihood; overrides carry approver and rationale.

## Troubleshooting

- Reconcile entity, period and unit when totals differ.
- Separate source fact, calculation and management judgement.
- Preserve the raw evidence and factor version.
- Escalate severe impacts even when likelihood or survey ranking is low.

## Cleanup

Remove personal and confidential data, retain source citations and hand over only the final learner artifact.
