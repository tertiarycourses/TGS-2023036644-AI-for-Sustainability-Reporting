# Activity 2 Map the reporting boundary and value chain

**Learning outcome:** LO1  
**Duration:** 50 minutes  
**Real use case:** Singtel Group  

## Objective

Build an entity and value-chain boundary that does not hide significant impacts.

## Files

- `case-study.pdf` - scenario, evidence and discussion tasks
- `quiz.pdf` - five open-ended knowledge checks
- `worksheet.csv` - working evidence register

## Inputs

Singtel Sustainability Report 2025, entity register and value-chain worksheet

## Detailed procedure

1. List controlled entities and reporting locations.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
2. Reconcile the list to the financial reporting scope.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
3. Map upstream, own-operation and downstream activities.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
4. Place actual and potential impacts on the map.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
5. Document exclusions, evidence gaps and restatement triggers.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.

## Expected output

Boundary register and value-chain impact map

## Verification

Every entity and impact has an inclusion decision, rationale, owner and evidence source.

## Troubleshooting

- Reconcile entity, period and unit when totals differ.
- Separate source fact, calculation and management judgement.
- Preserve the raw evidence and factor version.
- Escalate severe impacts even when likelihood or survey ranking is low.

## Cleanup

Remove personal and confidential data, retain source citations and hand over only the final learner artifact.
