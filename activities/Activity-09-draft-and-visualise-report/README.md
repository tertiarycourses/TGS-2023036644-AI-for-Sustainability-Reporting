# Activity 9 Draft and visualise a material-topic section

**Learning outcome:** LO3  
**Duration:** 70 minutes  
**Real use case:** CDL decarbonisation topic  

## Objective

Turn controlled data into a balanced GRI 3-3 topic section and decision-useful chart.

## Files

- `case-study.pdf` - scenario, evidence and discussion tasks
- `quiz.pdf` - five open-ended knowledge checks
- `worksheet.csv` - working evidence register

## Inputs

Topic fiche, KPI table, claim register and chart template

## Detailed procedure

1. State the impact and affected stakeholders.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
2. Describe policy, actions and due-diligence process.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
3. Compare result to baseline and target.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
4. Select line, bar, waterfall or table based on the question.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
5. Draft balanced narrative for progress, shortfall and corrective action.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.

## Expected output

Two-page topic section with editable chart and evidence links

## Verification

Chart reconciles to the table; narrative reports both achievement and material shortfall.

## Troubleshooting

- Reconcile entity, period and unit when totals differ.
- Separate source fact, calculation and management judgement.
- Preserve the raw evidence and factor version.
- Escalate severe impacts even when likelihood or survey ranking is low.

## Cleanup

Remove personal and confidential data, retain source citations and hand over only the final learner artifact.
