# Activity 4 Test a GRI claim and build the content index

**Learning outcome:** LO2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Real use case:** DBS Sustainability Report 2025  

## Objective

Determine in-accordance or with-reference status and create a working GRI content index.

## Files

- `case-study.pdf` - scenario, evidence and discussion tasks
- `quiz.pdf` - five open-ended knowledge checks
- `worksheet.csv` - working evidence register

## Inputs

GRI 1 nine-requirement checklist, DBS report and index template

## Detailed procedure

1. Test the report against all nine requirements.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
2. Record evidence location for each result.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
3. Choose the defensible statement of use.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
4. Create content-index entries for GRI 2 and GRI 3.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
5. Log omissions with reason, explanation and action date.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.

## Expected output

Claim-readiness checklist and content-index extract

## Verification

The claim wording matches the checklist; every index row resolves to an exact location or valid omission.

## Troubleshooting

- Reconcile entity, period and unit when totals differ.
- Separate source fact, calculation and management judgement.
- Preserve the raw evidence and factor version.
- Escalate severe impacts even when likelihood or survey ranking is low.

## Cleanup

Remove personal and confidential data, retain source citations and hand over only the final learner artifact.
