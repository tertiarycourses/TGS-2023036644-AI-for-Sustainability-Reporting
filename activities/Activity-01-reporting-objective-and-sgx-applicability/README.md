# Activity 1 Set the reporting objective and SGX applicability

**Learning outcome:** LO1  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Real use case:** City Developments Limited  

## Objective

Write a decision-useful reporting objective and determine which SGX 711A/711B components apply.

## Files

- `case-study.pdf` - scenario, evidence and discussion tasks
- `quiz.pdf` - five open-ended knowledge checks
- `worksheet.csv` - working evidence register

## Inputs

CDL reporting page, SGX Rules 711A/711B, applicability worksheet

## Detailed procedure

1. Read the public report profile and identify report users.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
2. Write one decision each user must make.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
3. Complete the 711A timing and 711B component checklist.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
4. Draft an objective naming audience, decision, period and boundary.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
5. Peer-review the objective against accuracy, balance and verifiability.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.

## Expected output

Approved objective statement and applicability memo

## Verification

All six 711B components are marked applicable/not applicable with evidence; the objective names audience, decision, period and boundary.

## Troubleshooting

- Reconcile entity, period and unit when totals differ.
- Separate source fact, calculation and management judgement.
- Preserve the raw evidence and factor version.
- Escalate severe impacts even when likelihood or survey ranking is low.

## Cleanup

Remove personal and confidential data, retain source citations and hand over only the final learner artifact.
