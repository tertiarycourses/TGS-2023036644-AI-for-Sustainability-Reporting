# Activity 8 Build social disclosure controls

**Learning outcome:** LO2  
**Duration:** 65 minutes  
**Real use case:** Regional logistics workforce  

## Objective

Prepare employment, safety, diversity and privacy metrics without denominator or confidentiality errors.

## Files

- `case-study.pdf` - scenario, evidence and discussion tasks
- `quiz.pdf` - five open-ended knowledge checks
- `worksheet.csv` - working evidence register

## Inputs

HR roster, hours, incident, training and complaint datasets

## Detailed procedure

1. Define employee and worker populations.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
2. Calculate hire, turnover and injury rates.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
3. Reconcile training coverage to eligible population.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
4. Aggregate diversity categories and suppress small cells.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
5. Classify complaints as inquiry, allegation or substantiated case.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.

## Expected output

Social KPI tables and privacy review record

## Verification

Numerators and denominators use the same population; no small cell reveals an individual.

## Troubleshooting

- Reconcile entity, period and unit when totals differ.
- Separate source fact, calculation and management judgement.
- Preserve the raw evidence and factor version.
- Escalate severe impacts even when likelihood or survey ranking is low.

## Cleanup

Remove personal and confidential data, retain source citations and hand over only the final learner artifact.
