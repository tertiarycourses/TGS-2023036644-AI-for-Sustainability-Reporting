# Activity 6 Assemble a GRI 2 governance evidence pack

**Learning outcome:** LO2  
**Duration:** 55 minutes  
**Real use case:** CDL sustainability governance  

## Objective

Draft governance disclosures from verifiable oversight evidence.

## Files

- `case-study.pdf` - scenario, evidence and discussion tasks
- `quiz.pdf` - five open-ended knowledge checks
- `worksheet.csv` - working evidence register

## Inputs

CDL governance page, disclosure checklist and evidence index

## Detailed procedure

1. Map Board, committee, management and operational roles.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
2. Identify decision frequency and escalation route.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
3. Capture competence, conflicts and remuneration links.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
4. Match each claim to terms of reference or minutes.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
5. Draft a concise governance disclosure with evidence references.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.

## Expected output

Governance map, evidence index and GRI 2 draft

## Verification

Every oversight claim names an actor, decision, cadence and evidence record.

## Troubleshooting

- Reconcile entity, period and unit when totals differ.
- Separate source fact, calculation and management judgement.
- Preserve the raw evidence and factor version.
- Escalate severe impacts even when likelihood or survey ranking is low.

## Cleanup

Remove personal and confidential data, retain source citations and hand over only the final learner artifact.
