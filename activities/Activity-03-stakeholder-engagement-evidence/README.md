# Activity 3 Design stakeholder engagement evidence

**Learning outcome:** LO2  
**Duration:** 55 minutes  
**Real use case:** CDL materiality process  

## Objective

Plan engagement that captures affected-stakeholder perspectives and closes the feedback loop.

## Files

- `case-study.pdf` - scenario, evidence and discussion tasks
- `quiz.pdf` - five open-ended knowledge checks
- `worksheet.csv` - working evidence register

## Inputs

CDL materiality reporting, stakeholder register and interview guide

## Detailed procedure

1. Identify stakeholder groups and affected interests.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
2. Score influence and degree affected separately.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
3. Choose survey, interview, workshop or grievance evidence.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
4. Record issues raised and management responses.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
5. Draft the disclosure of what changed and what did not.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.

## Expected output

Stakeholder map, engagement log and disclosure paragraph

## Verification

Each material issue traces to a stakeholder source, management decision and feedback response.

## Troubleshooting

- Reconcile entity, period and unit when totals differ.
- Separate source fact, calculation and management judgement.
- Preserve the raw evidence and factor version.
- Escalate severe impacts even when likelihood or survey ranking is low.

## Cleanup

Remove personal and confidential data, retain source citations and hand over only the final learner artifact.
