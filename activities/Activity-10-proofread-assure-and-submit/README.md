# Activity 10 Proofread, assure and submit the report

**Learning outcome:** LO3  
**Duration:** 75 minutes  
**Real use case:** Board release simulation  

## Objective

Execute numeric, content, approval and publication controls on a near-final report.

## Files

- `case-study.pdf` - scenario, evidence and discussion tasks
- `quiz.pdf` - five open-ended knowledge checks
- `worksheet.csv` - working evidence register

## Inputs

Near-final report, content index, issue log and approval checklist

## Detailed procedure

1. Cross-foot tables and reconcile repeated figures.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
2. Validate units, dates, entity names, codes, links and omissions.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
3. Close critical findings and document remaining limitations.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
4. Bind the approved version to Board sign-off and checksum.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
5. Run post-release link, filename and channel-consistency checks.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.

## Expected output

Closed issue log, approval record and submission inventory

## Verification

Zero open critical findings; the approved hash matches every released copy and every content-index link resolves.

## Troubleshooting

- Reconcile entity, period and unit when totals differ.
- Separate source fact, calculation and management judgement.
- Preserve the raw evidence and factor version.
- Escalate severe impacts even when likelihood or survey ranking is low.

## Cleanup

Remove personal and confidential data, retain source citations and hand over only the final learner artifact.
