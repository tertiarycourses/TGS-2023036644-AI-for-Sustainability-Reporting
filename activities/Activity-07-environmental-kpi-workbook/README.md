# Activity 7 Calculate environmental GRI metrics

**Learning outcome:** LO2  
**Duration:** 80 minutes  
**Real use case:** Singapore manufacturing data pack  

## Objective

Calculate energy, Scope 1/2 GHG, water and waste metrics with transparent boundaries and factors.

## Files

- `case-study.pdf` - scenario, evidence and discussion tasks
- `quiz.pdf` - five open-ended knowledge checks
- `worksheet.csv` - working evidence register

## Inputs

Meter, fuel, water and waste CSV files with factor register

## Detailed procedure

1. Validate units, periods and site coverage.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
2. Convert energy to GJ and calculate emissions as activity x factor.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
3. Reconcile water withdrawal, discharge and consumption.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
4. Classify waste by composition and destination.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.
5. Publish absolute and intensity metrics with assumptions.
   - Record the source, period, boundary, owner and evidence link in `worksheet.csv`.
   - If evidence is missing, log the limitation and resolution date; do not estimate silently.

## Expected output

Controlled KPI workbook and four disclosure tables

## Verification

Recalculation matches reported values; units, factors, boundaries and denominators are disclosed.

## Troubleshooting

- Reconcile entity, period and unit when totals differ.
- Separate source fact, calculation and management judgement.
- Preserve the raw evidence and factor version.
- Escalate severe impacts even when likelihood or survey ranking is low.

## Cleanup

Remove personal and confidential data, retain source citations and hand over only the final learner artifact.
