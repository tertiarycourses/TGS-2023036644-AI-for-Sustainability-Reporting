# Learner Guide Sustainability Reporting and GRI Standards
**Course code:** TGS-2023036644  
**Version:** 3.0  

## Learning Outcomes
- LO1: Identify objectives of sustainability reports in accordance with organisational procedures.
- LO2: Structure and collaborate with stakeholders on sustainability reporting based on GRI Standards.
- LO3: Proofread and submit sustainability reports with data displayed effectively to relevant stakeholders.

## Activity 1 Set the reporting objective and SGX applicability
Write a decision-useful reporting objective and determine which SGX 711A/711B components apply.

**Inputs:** CDL reporting page, SGX Rules 711A/711B, applicability worksheet

### Detailed Procedure
1. Read the public report profile and identify report users.
2. Write one decision each user must make.
3. Complete the 711A timing and 711B component checklist.
4. Draft an objective naming audience, decision, period and boundary.
5. Peer-review the objective against accuracy, balance and verifiability.

**Verification:** All six 711B components are marked applicable/not applicable with evidence; the objective names audience, decision, period and boundary.

## Activity 2 Map the reporting boundary and value chain
Build an entity and value-chain boundary that does not hide significant impacts.

**Inputs:** Singtel Sustainability Report 2025, entity register and value-chain worksheet

### Detailed Procedure
1. List controlled entities and reporting locations.
2. Reconcile the list to the financial reporting scope.
3. Map upstream, own-operation and downstream activities.
4. Place actual and potential impacts on the map.
5. Document exclusions, evidence gaps and restatement triggers.

**Verification:** Every entity and impact has an inclusion decision, rationale, owner and evidence source.

## Activity 3 Design stakeholder engagement evidence
Plan engagement that captures affected-stakeholder perspectives and closes the feedback loop.

**Inputs:** CDL materiality reporting, stakeholder register and interview guide

### Detailed Procedure
1. Identify stakeholder groups and affected interests.
2. Score influence and degree affected separately.
3. Choose survey, interview, workshop or grievance evidence.
4. Record issues raised and management responses.
5. Draft the disclosure of what changed and what did not.

**Verification:** Each material issue traces to a stakeholder source, management decision and feedback response.

## Activity 4 Test a GRI claim and build the content index
Determine in-accordance or with-reference status and create a working GRI content index.

**Inputs:** GRI 1 nine-requirement checklist, DBS report and index template

### Detailed Procedure
1. Test the report against all nine requirements.
2. Record evidence location for each result.
3. Choose the defensible statement of use.
4. Create content-index entries for GRI 2 and GRI 3.
5. Log omissions with reason, explanation and action date.

**Verification:** The claim wording matches the checklist; every index row resolves to an exact location or valid omission.

## Activity 5 Determine material topics with GRI 3
Identify, assess and prioritise impacts using the GRI 3 significance process.

**Inputs:** CDL topic list, impact register and severity worksheet

### Detailed Procedure
1. Review organisational context and business relationships.
2. Write impact statements using cause-pathway-subject-consequence.
3. Score scale, scope and irremediable character.
4. Assess likelihood only for potential impacts.
5. Apply the threshold and document governance validation.

**Verification:** High-severity human-rights impacts are not screened out by low likelihood; overrides carry approver and rationale.

## Activity 6 Assemble a GRI 2 governance evidence pack
Draft governance disclosures from verifiable oversight evidence.

**Inputs:** CDL governance page, disclosure checklist and evidence index

### Detailed Procedure
1. Map Board, committee, management and operational roles.
2. Identify decision frequency and escalation route.
3. Capture competence, conflicts and remuneration links.
4. Match each claim to terms of reference or minutes.
5. Draft a concise governance disclosure with evidence references.

**Verification:** Every oversight claim names an actor, decision, cadence and evidence record.

## Activity 7 Calculate environmental GRI metrics
Calculate energy, Scope 1/2 GHG, water and waste metrics with transparent boundaries and factors.

**Inputs:** Meter, fuel, water and waste CSV files with factor register

### Detailed Procedure
1. Validate units, periods and site coverage.
2. Convert energy to GJ and calculate emissions as activity x factor.
3. Reconcile water withdrawal, discharge and consumption.
4. Classify waste by composition and destination.
5. Publish absolute and intensity metrics with assumptions.

**Verification:** Recalculation matches reported values; units, factors, boundaries and denominators are disclosed.

## Activity 8 Build social disclosure controls
Prepare employment, safety, diversity and privacy metrics without denominator or confidentiality errors.

**Inputs:** HR roster, hours, incident, training and complaint datasets

### Detailed Procedure
1. Define employee and worker populations.
2. Calculate hire, turnover and injury rates.
3. Reconcile training coverage to eligible population.
4. Aggregate diversity categories and suppress small cells.
5. Classify complaints as inquiry, allegation or substantiated case.

**Verification:** Numerators and denominators use the same population; no small cell reveals an individual.

## Activity 9 Draft and visualise a material-topic section
Turn controlled data into a balanced GRI 3-3 topic section and decision-useful chart.

**Inputs:** Topic fiche, KPI table, claim register and chart template

### Detailed Procedure
1. State the impact and affected stakeholders.
2. Describe policy, actions and due-diligence process.
3. Compare result to baseline and target.
4. Select line, bar, waterfall or table based on the question.
5. Draft balanced narrative for progress, shortfall and corrective action.

**Verification:** Chart reconciles to the table; narrative reports both achievement and material shortfall.

## Activity 10 Proofread, assure and submit the report
Execute numeric, content, approval and publication controls on a near-final report.

**Inputs:** Near-final report, content index, issue log and approval checklist

### Detailed Procedure
1. Cross-foot tables and reconcile repeated figures.
2. Validate units, dates, entity names, codes, links and omissions.
3. Close critical findings and document remaining limitations.
4. Bind the approved version to Board sign-off and checksum.
5. Run post-release link, filename and channel-consistency checks.

**Verification:** Zero open critical findings; the approved hash matches every released copy and every content-index link resolves.
